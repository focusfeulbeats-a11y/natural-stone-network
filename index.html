AI Project Planner

Tell us what you want. We'll turn it into a brief.

No industry knowledge required. Describe your project in your own words, and the planner asks the right follow-up questions — material, application, timeline, budget shape — then structures it into a brief professionals can quote against.

  • Understands stone types, finishes and applications automatically
  • Asks only the questions that matter for your project
  • Matches your brief to suitably specialised, local professionals
  • You review and send — nothing is shared without your say-so
